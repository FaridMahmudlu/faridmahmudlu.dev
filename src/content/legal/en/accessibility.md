## Commitment

I want this website to be usable by everyone, regardless of device, ability or assistive technology. It is designed and built to meet the **Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, level AA**.

## What has been done

- **Semantic structure.** Landmarks, a logical heading hierarchy, lists and descriptive link text.
- **Keyboard access.** Every interactive element can be reached and operated with a keyboard, with a clearly visible focus indicator and a “skip to content” link.
- **Contrast.** Body text meets at least a 4.5:1 contrast ratio against the background.
- **Motion.** If your system asks for reduced motion, smooth scrolling, reveal animations and particle transitions are disabled. A **Pause motion** button stops all continuous animation (WCAG 2.2.2).
- **Decorative 3D.** The WebGL particle scene is purely decorative and hidden from assistive technology. All information it accompanies is available as text.
- **Works without JavaScript or WebGL.** All content and navigation remain available if scripts or 3D graphics are blocked or unsupported.
- **Language.** Each page declares its language (English, Azerbaijani or Hungarian), and links to other languages are marked accordingly.
- **Responsive and zoomable.** Layouts adapt from 320 px wide screens upward and support text zoom up to 200%.
- **No flashing content** and no time limits.

## Known limitations

- The particle visuals have no text alternative by design, because they carry no information that is not also present in text.
- External websites linked from the portfolio, including product websites, are outside my control and may not meet the same standard.

## Compatibility

The website is tested with current versions of Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari, and with the NVDA and VoiceOver screen readers.

## Feedback

If you encounter a barrier, please email me with the page address and a short description of the problem. I aim to respond within a week and to fix confirmed issues promptly.

## Assessment

This statement is based on a self-assessment carried out in October 2026.
