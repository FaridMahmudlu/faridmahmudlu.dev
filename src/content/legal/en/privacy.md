This policy explains how personal data is processed when you visit **faridmahmudlu.dev** (the “website”). It follows Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (the General Data Protection Regulation, “GDPR”) and Hungarian Act CXII of 2011 on the Right of Informational Self-Determination and on Freedom of Information (the “Info Act”).

## Summary

- This website sets **no cookies** for analytics, advertising or personalisation and uses no local storage.
- There are **no ad networks, social-media pixels, fingerprinting or third-party embeds**.
- Fonts and scripts are **self-hosted** — your browser does not contact Google Fonts or any other CDN.
- Visitor statistics are **aggregate and cookieless**.
- If you email me, your message is used **only to reply**.

## Who is responsible

The controller is **Farid Mahmudlu**, a private individual based in Budapest, Hungary. The contact email address is shown at the end of this page and in the contact section of the homepage. A data protection officer has not been appointed because none is required (Art. 37 GDPR).

## What is processed, why, and on which legal basis

### 1. Delivering and protecting the website

When you open a page, your browser necessarily transmits technical data to the hosting provider: IP address, date and time of the request, requested URL, referring page, user-agent string (browser and operating system), response status and amount of data transferred.

- **Purpose:** to deliver the website, keep it stable and secure, and detect and mitigate abuse such as denial-of-service attacks or malicious bots.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — my legitimate interest in operating a secure and reliable website.
- **Retention:** this data is processed by Cloudflare on my behalf and retained only for the short periods defined in Cloudflare’s policies. I do not receive raw access logs and do not combine this data with other sources.

To protect the site, Cloudflare may set **strictly necessary security cookies** (for example `__cf_bm` or `cf_clearance`), only when needed to distinguish people from automated traffic or after a security check. These cookies do not track you across websites. They are exempt from consent under Art. 5(3) of the ePrivacy Directive, as implemented in Hungary by Section 155(4) of Act C of 2003 on Electronic Communications. Legal basis: Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR.

### 2. Visitor statistics

I use **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to understand which pages are read and how fast they load. It does not use cookies or local storage and does not fingerprint visitors by IP address, user agent or any other data. Collected data is aggregate: page path, referrer, browser, operating system and device type, country (derived from the IP address, which is not stored by the analytics product) and performance metrics such as Core Web Vitals.

- **Purpose:** improving content and performance.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — my legitimate interest in understanding, in aggregate, how the website is used.

### 3. Contacting me by email

If you email me, I process your email address, name, the content of your message and its metadata.

- **Purpose:** answering your enquiry.
- **Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR where your message concerns steps prior to entering a contract (for example a job or project enquiry); otherwise Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR — my legitimate interest in replying to messages addressed to me.
- **Retention:** as long as the conversation requires, and deleted no later than two years after our last exchange, unless a longer retention is required by law.
- **Delivery:** messages sent to the address on this website are forwarded by Cloudflare Email Routing, which does not store them, to my mailbox hosted by Google (Gmail), where they are stored.

### 4. Downloading the CV

The CV is a static PDF file. Downloading it creates only the technical data described in section 1. The public version does not contain my phone number.

## Cookies and local storage

Apart from the strictly necessary security cookies described in section 1, this website does not set cookies and does not use `localStorage`, `sessionStorage` or `IndexedDB`. For this reason there is no cookie banner.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- access your personal data (Art. 15);
- have inaccurate data rectified (Art. 16);
- have your data erased (Art. 17);
- restrict processing (Art. 18);
- receive your data in a portable format (Art. 20);
- **object** at any time to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21).

To exercise these rights, email me. I will reply without undue delay and within one month at the latest (Art. 12(3) GDPR). Please note that I usually cannot identify individual visitors from technical or statistical data unless you provide additional information (Art. 11 GDPR).
