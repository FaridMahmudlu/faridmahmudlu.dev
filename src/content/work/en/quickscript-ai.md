## Context

Short-form video is won or lost in the first seconds. QuickScript AI gives creators a structured starting point instead of a blank page.

## What I built

- **A Node.js / Express backend** that prompts Llama 4 Maverick through OpenRouter.
- **Structured output:** Hook → Body → CTA, plus production notes and voice cues.
- **Platform-specific formatting** for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts.
- **Six languages:** English, Azerbaijani, Turkish, Arabic, Spanish and Russian.
- **Tone controls**, copy to clipboard, `.txt` export and a local script history.

## Status

Open source under the MIT licence. Accounts and payments are interface prototypes; scripts are stored locally in the browser.
